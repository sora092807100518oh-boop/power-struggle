# StudyVerse Design Direction

## Product interpretation

StudyVerse is designed as a **student-first learning cockpit**, not a generic dashboard. The experience starts at a compact PIN entry and unfolds into a calm, luminous study space where the student’s next action is always visually obvious. The initial implementation will make the core learning flows interactive and will use clearly labeled setup states for external services that are not yet configured.

## Visual system

The product uses a **midnight observatory** direction: a deep navy-to-indigo atmospheric ground, restrained cyan and violet light, translucent glass panels, and bright paper cards for active recall. Japanese interface copy is set in Noto Sans JP for readability, while the brand uses a more editorial display treatment. The primary mobile composition has deliberate vertical rhythm, dense but legible status information, and a fixed bottom navigation system.

## Layout and interaction

The home screen uses a stacked command-center layout: top utility bar, weekly progress summary, three fixed-height swipeable panels (calendar, creature growth, mission), then one-tap entries to StudyJournal and AI Select 10. Vocabulary work uses clear bilingual controls and an opaque workspace that provides strong contrast. Card practice is intentionally paper-like so that it becomes a tactile visual contrast to the ambient space setting.

## Accessibility and motion

Every control will retain a visible focus style, text contrast will be evaluated against the rendered background, and animation will be limited to transform/opacity transitions under 300ms. The experience will respect reduced-motion preferences and will offer buttons in addition to swiping for card practice.

## Content policy

No customer reviews, ratings, testimonials, or fabricated user-generated content will be shown. Sample study material is presented only as functional learning content and is visually distinguished from user data.
